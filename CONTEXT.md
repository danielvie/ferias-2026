# Belgium trip planning

A shared trip brings together the plans and practical details for a group visiting Belgium.

## Language

**Trip**: A named journey with dates, an ordered route of city visits, and invited members.

**Checkpoint**, also called a **city visit**: One stop on the route with its own activities. A trip can visit the same city more than once, as separate checkpoints.
_Avoid_: Treating a city name as the identity of a visit.

**Trip member**: A named participant with a stable identity and role within a trip. Joining through the trip join link creates a new member. It is not a verified personal account.

**Member name**: A member's editable display label. Renaming a member does not make them a different member, and two members can have the same name.

**Change history**: A record of who added, changed or removed trip data, under the name they used at the time. Recording an action does not mean that member paid for it or owes its cost.

**Trip join link**, also called a **trip invitation link**: A reusable URL allowing each visitor to enter a name and join as an editor. People use the same invitation but get separate member identities. It does not reopen an existing member merely because their name matches.

**Member link**: A private URL granting its holder a particular trip role. People sharing the same member link share that member's identity and access.

**Owner link**: The private member link that grants trip management and control of other links. It is also the owner's way back without signing in.

**Activity**: A saved thing to do, such as an attraction, walk or restaurant visit. It belongs to one city visit, can remain unscheduled, and keeps its identity when moved.

**Place**: The saved point of interest and planning details of an activity.

**Event**: A timed plan on a trip day, optionally linked to a saved place.
_Avoid_: Using "place" for a scheduled appointment.

**Transfer**: A move between two cities on a specific trip day, with its own departure checklist.

**Checklist**: A named, shared list of items for the trip. Its items can be pending or completed and may have an assigned member or shared label. A trip-wide checklist is not tied to a city or departure.

**City checklist**: A named checklist attached to one checkpoint, either for arriving or leaving. It stays with that visit when the route is reordered. Creating one from another checklist copies its items with new identities and resets them to pending; later edits and completion are independent.
_Avoid_: Treating the city name or a pair of neighboring checkpoints as its identity.

**Packing list**: The initial checklist of items to bring on the whole trip. Packing an item does not complete a departure check.

**Departure checklist**: Checks to resolve before one transfer. Each transfer starts unchecked, independently of earlier transfers.

**Private note**: A note only its author's member identity can read, even if another member owns the trip. Joining through the common trip join link does not grant another member's private notes. Anyone holding an identity-specific member link shares that identity's access.

**Shared note**: A note visible to trip members. Family links do not include notes.

**Family link**: A private URL granting read-only access to the trip summary and selected itinerary/places, without signing in or exposing private travel details.
